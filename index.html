How it plays

1

Change one letter

You and your rival share one word. Swap a letter to make a new real word: COLD → CORD → CARD.

2

Be quick

Faster answers earn more energy. Build combos, set traps, and first to 120 wins.

3

K.O. or Dead End

Leave your rival with no move and you win on the spot. Then learn what every word means.

See it in action

Why you'll love it

Comic-book action

  • Tiles slam down with BAM!, ZAP! and POP!
  • Combos, ON FIRE streaks and a slow-motion K.O.
  • Turn the effects down any time for a calm game.

Learn as you play

  • The word in play shows its meaning on the board.
  • Words you missed wait for you in a quick quiz.
  • Watch your learned-words count grow.

Play your way

  • Duel a bot that grows with your rank.
  • Two players on one phone, side by side or pass-the-phone.
  • Two phones on the same Wi-Fi, no internet needed.

Fair and family-friendly

  • Free, with small family-safe banners, never during a match.
  • Nothing to buy. No account. Works offline.
  • For players aged 13 and over.

Be one of the first players

WordMaster is in its final test on Google Play. Join free and help shape the game:

  1. On your Android phone, join the WordMaster Testers group with your Google account.
  2. Open the test link (posted in the group), tap Become a tester, then install from Google Play.
  3. Play a few matches now and then, and stay in for 14 days. That's all!
